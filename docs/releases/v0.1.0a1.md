# Nemora 0.1.0a1

Published 2026-10-30.

## Highlights

- `nemora.inventory.v1` ingest schema, HPS migration, and FAIB/FIA helpers.
- Plot selection with deterministic train/hold-out splits and tree-detail
  resolution.
- Empirical and parametric stand validation with hold-out metrics, weighted DBH
  KS, species TV distance, and bootstrap intervals.
- Optional `nemora.lidar` raster sampling and canopy metrics.
- LidarBC DEM/DSM materialization through FEMIC and CHM/slope context.
- FHOPS `fhops.tree.inventory.v1` export, scenario pinning, independent yield
  checks, and multi-block scenario catalog.

## Validation status

- Gavin Lake is the primary validation target.
- The selected empirical model improves held-out DBH/height/KS over the pooled
  Weibull, but density/BA remain imperfect.
- Malcolm Knapp regional transfer is limited; age stratification improves
  distributional metrics but worsens density/BA.
- Four LidarBC plot gaps remain explicit.
- Synthetic stands are not surveyed tree maps.

## Limits

No operational, financial, or regulatory claim is made. The FHOPS handoff is a
reference integration, not an operational plan. The independent yield estimate
is not a taper model.
